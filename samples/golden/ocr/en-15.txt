Robert Lee Stowe was born April 5, 1866 in a small log cabin in Southpoint Township about two miles below the present town of Belmont. He started his education in a little building at the edge of Smith's graveyard. He attended many schools in this vicinity and graduated from Bell's Military School at Kings Mountain in 1886. In the fall of 1886 he taught school at New Hope. In the spring of 1888 he taught in the public schools of what is now Belmont. In January of 1889 he taught at the old South Point school house.

Mr. Stowe went into the mercantile business with Mr. W. B. Puett November 6, 1889. A little later Mr. Puett's interest was bought by Mr. S. P. Stowe and the store was known as Stowe Brothers Company.

Mr. Stowe organized the first cotton mill in Belmont in 1901, naming it after Major Chronicle. He organized the second mill in 1906, the Imperial Yarn Mills, Inc. Other mills were organized by him and his brother S. P. Stowe. Mr. A. C. Lineberger joined them early in the twentieth century and this trio organized and built many of Belmont's mills.

The Bank of Belmont was organized and chartered by Mr. Stowe and associates in 1907. He has served faithfully as a Commissioner in Gaston County forty odd years, being chairman of this Board for over twenty-five years.

Mr. Stowe has been treasurer of the First Presbyterian Church of Belmont for almost sixty years. He is listed in Who's Who of America.
