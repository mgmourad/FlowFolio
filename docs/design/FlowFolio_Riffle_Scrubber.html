The strip is a front view of the book's fore edge, like a cross-section seen from the front: front cover board on the left, back cover board on the right, and every leaf standing between them edge-on. Chapter bleed tabs print as stepped colour bars on the edge. The red ribbon hangs from a pin at your position; it is the only thing that riffles.

Try this

Slide the ribbon left or right: the pin opens a gap in the fore edge, leaves spring across it bowing with their own speed, and the loupe riffles miniature spreads. Release to land with a multi-sheet page turn. Tap a flag on top to jump straight to that bookmark (hover one with a mouse to preview it). Flags never drag, and tapping the page block itself only nudges the ribbon. After the auto-hide delay the controls fade and the spread grows to a clean full-screen book; tap the middle of the page to bring them back.

Mapping to Compose

Draw the block in one Canvas, batching leaf curves into ~16 shade buckets of Path so a frame is about 20 draw calls, not 421.

Keep leaf angles and velocities in two FloatArrays integrated inside a withFrameNanos loop; per-leaf Animatables would be far too heavy.

Gestures via pointerInput + awaitEachGesture, with separate hit regions: ribbon (drag), flags (tap only). Haptics: SEGMENT_FREQUENT_TICK while riffling and CONFIRM on chapters (API 34+, CLOCK_TICK fallback). Paper flicks from a small SoundPool.

Auto-hide is a coroutine timer reset on every interaction; immersive mode hides system bars via WindowInsetsControllerCompat and animates the book with graphicsLayer { scaleX; scaleY }.

Loupe thumbnails come from the page bitmap cache at low resolution; the riffle flips use graphicsLayer { rotationY; cameraDistance }.
