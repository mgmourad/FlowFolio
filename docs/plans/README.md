# Plans and sign-offs

These are the project's working records, kept as they were written: a plan before each milestone, and a sign-off
report after it saying what was built, what was measured and what was left open.

They are addressed to the product owner and written in the second person ("what you can try"), because that is
what they were — delivery reports, not documentation. They are kept unedited rather than tidied up, so the record
of what was actually decided, and when, stays honest.

For the current state of the project, read [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). For why things are the way they are,
read the [ADRs](../adr). For what changed, read [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md).
